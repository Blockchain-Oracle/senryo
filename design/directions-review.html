Metropolis · Track 01 · mobile trading app with a card

Four design directions to choose from

Same seven screens in each direction: home, markets, trade ticket, Face ID confirm, card, fund, and loading, empty and error states. Every screen is built from real 21st.dev components, re-coloured per direction and rendered at iPhone width. Pick one, or combine parts, before a designer refines it.
