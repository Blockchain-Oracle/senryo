## 1. Senryo contracts: what is on chain

**Band types.** All five kinds already exist in the contract:
- Up and Down: above or below the open price K; a close exactly at K refunds.
- Range: between K−a and K+b, edges included.
- Moonshot: above K+x, the strike itself loses.
- Crash: below K−x, the strike itself loses.
- Evidence: `contracts/src/markets/MarketTypes.sol:58-62`; at most 8 bands per series (`:55`).

**A band definition** (`BandDef`) is `{uint8 kind, uint16 lowBps, uint16 highBps}`, with offsets in basis points of K (`MarketTypes.sol:142-148`). The validity rules (`BandMath.sol:93-98`):
- Up and Down carry no offsets.
- Range needs both `lowBps` and `highBps` above 0 and below 10,000, so every range must contain K.
- Moonshot and Crash use `lowBps` only.

**Menus** (`BandBook.sol:27-28, 42-48`):
- `addBand` appends to a series' menu and is admin-only.
- Entries can never be changed or removed, because tickets name a band by its index.
- Adding a band does **not** bump `configVersion`, so it is callable the moment it lands.
- `menuOf(seriesId)` reads the menu (`BandReserve.sol:138-140`).
- `setSigma` is bounded and does bump `configVersion` (`BandBook.sol:50-56`).

**Pricing** (`BandMath.sol:107-123`):
- Std dev = σ√τ, with τ never below 5 s (`:69-72`); z = ln(edge/spot) / std dev (`:75-83`).
- P(above edge) = 1 − Φ(z) (`:86-88`).
- Range = P(above low edge) − P(above high edge); Moonshot = P(above K+x); Crash = 1 − P(above K−x).
- Each band has an exact closed-form probability. There is no "touch at any time" band; everything settles on the close price.
- Edge prices are computed by `offsetE8`, which truncates (`:101-103`).

**Fill** (`BandBook.sol:197-225`):
- Probability is taken at the fill print, with τ = expiry − target (`:273-276`).
- **Refused, never clamped**, if probability is outside [minProb, maxProb] (`:202`).
- Price = probability + half-spread + surcharge, where surcharge = maxSurcharge × reservedByExpiry / maxExpiryReserved (`:203-204`).
- Refused if the payout is not above the stake (`:208`), below the signed limit (`:209`), or does not fit capacity (`:211`).
- `quoteOpen` is the view version of the same calculation (`BandReserve.sol:144-158`).

**Cash-out** (`BandBook.sol:229-260`) works the same for every kind:
- Bid = probability − half-spread, and proceeds are linear in shares.
- Refused outside [minProb, maxProb] (`:234`).
- So a deep out-of-the-money Moonshot (under 3%) or a near-certain Range (over 97%) cannot be cashed out.

**Settlement:** `settleWindow` decides each used band with `BandMath.outcome` (`BandReserve.sol:73-93`; rules at `BandMath.sol:127-141`).

**Capacity limits** (`BandPool.sol:113-118`):
- The pool must have the liquidity.
- Total exposure must stay within `maxExposureBps` of the pool.
- Each expiry has its own cap, **keyed by expiry time only** (`:41`). BTC, ETH and SOL windows ending at the same instant share one cap and one surcharge.
- Admin bounds on the parameters: `MarketTypes.sol:90-95`, enforced at `BandPool.sol:94-107`.

**What is registered.** `packages/config/src/catalog.ts:109-120` builds every series' menu in this order (`BAND_INDEX` at `:34`): Up, Down, Range ±0.5σ√τ, Moonshot and Crash at 1σ√τ (`:61-63`). The deploy script pushes each menu with `addBand` (`contracts/script/DeployMarkets.s.sol:142-169`).

**Live check.** I read `menuOf` on BandReserve `0xbcf5…30D4` for all 12 series (BTC, ETH, SOL × 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h). Series ids came from `Windows.seriesIdOf` (`Windows.sol:253-255`); for example BTC 1m is `0x321c0b35…aaf5`. All 12 match `contracts/script/catalog/10143.json` exactly:

## 2. Senryo app and packages

**Already generic, no change needed:**
- `packages/core/src/market/band-math.ts:89-123` mirrors the contract's maths for all five kinds bit for bit; `bandOutcome` is at `:110`.
- `band-quote.ts:36-60` (`quoteOpen` and `quoteClose`) works for any kind.

**Up/Down-only, needs changing:**
- **`packages/calls/src/quote.ts`:**
  - Inputs and results are hard-wired to `up` and `down` (`QuoteInput` `:117-118`, `Quotes` `:124-128`, `QuotePass` `:137-151`).
  - `quoteTick` prices only those two (`:172-173`).
  - With a call open, the overlay assumes Up/Down: `isDown` (`:201`), `side ±1` (`:209`), "winning" as spot > K or spot < K (`:216`), and the shaded zone `above`/`below` (`:220`).
  - `ChartLevel.kind` is only `"line" | "entry"` (`:36-42`), and `ChartOverlay.zone` has only those two values (`:53-54`).
  - `pricingFor` (`:90-107`) and `oddsLine` (`:157-163`) can be reused as they are.
- **Call flow:** `packages/calls/src/use-call-flow.ts:49-50, 112-135` takes `open(side: "up"|"down")` with `upBand`/`downBand` and labels "Up"/"Down".
- **Band lookup:**
  - Both apps pick Up/Down out of the menu by kind: `apps/web/src/features/terminal/useLiveQuote.ts:53-54, 78-79` and `apps/mobile/.../useLiveQuote.ts:47-48`.
  - Selection is by band index (`bands?.[position.band]`, `:56`).
- **Charts:**
  - Both charts shade only one side of K: `apps/web/.../chart/chart-engine.ts:210-213` and `apps/mobile/.../chart/draw.ts:203-206`.
  - `ReactionPosition` assumes ±1 and a single losing line, K (`packages/calls/src/reactions.ts:10-21`).
- **Labels and other surfaces:**
  - Names come from `SIDE` = {0 Up, 1 Down, 2 Range, 3 Moonshot, 4 Crash} (`packages/core/src/market/call-format.ts:21`), and the keeper uses `BAND_INDEX` (`services/keeper/src/results.ts:12, 34`).
  - The crowd split shows only Up% and Down% of the total, so it won't add to 100% once Range or Moonshot stake exists (`packages/calls/src/receipt.ts:126-130`).
- **API:**
  - There is no quote endpoint; quoting happens on the device.
  - `/v1/markets/catalog` serves the menu from the config's `bandMenu`, **not from chain `menuOf`** (`services/api/src/routes/markets.ts:95`).
  - The schema already accepts all five kinds and band indices 0–7 (`packages/api-client/src/routes/markets.ts:23, 29-34, 87`).
- **Indexer:** it does not index `BandAdded` or `SigmaSet` (`indexer/config.yaml:19-37`). `bandStake` is per index (`schema.graphql:31`).

## 3. The references

**Owarine Moonshot:**
- Page `/games/moonshot` on web and mobile (`web/src/components/shell/nav.ts:125-131`, `mobile/src/nav/items.ts:50`): "Aim for a distant price target."
- The user picks a direction (long or short) and a multiple from 2, 3, 5, 10 or 25 on an aim ladder: "Up is long, down is short. Further out pays more." (`packages/core/src/range/moonshot.ts:18, 24`; copy at `web/src/features/games/moonshot/copy.ts:14-41`).
- The **strike is solved per quote**, so the multiple stays fixed (`moonshot.ts:101-141`). Target probability = 1/(multiple × (1 + margin)) (`:54-57`).
- It is booked as a Range whose far edge is unreachable (4× the open, or 1 cent) (`:79-81, 157-162`).
- Copy: "long · above $X" (`copy.ts:70`); "Long wins if the Window closes **at or above** the level" (`:20`), i.e. strike included.
- Payout caps per rung: 25× capped at 100 units, 10× at 200 (`moonshot.ts:169`).

**Owarine Range:**
- Page `/games/range` ("Pick the band where price should finish", `nav.ts:117-124`), plus a range mode in the terminal ticket (`web/src/features/markets/ticket/useTicketComposer.ts:75`).
- Free-form low and high bounds; Tight/Medium/Wide presets of ±15/30/55 dollars at BTC $77k on 5m, scaled by cadence and price (`web/src/features/range/presets.ts:12-35`).
- A centre slider and ±$5 steps (`copy.ts:30-62`).
- Inside or outside on the game page; the terminal ticket offers inside only (`BandControl.tsx:16`).
- Copy: "Tighter pays more", "X% chance it lands inside", "Place RANGE $X to $Y →" (`copy.ts:18-20, 80, 110`).
- Pricing is a band probability shifted by the order book's centre, with a multiplicative margin (`packages/core/src/range/pricing.ts:66-72, 117-120`).
- Settles on the close price, edges inclusive. **There is no cash-out**: the Daml contract has only settle, claim and refund (`daml/abu-pm-tickets/daml/PM/Tickets/Range.daml:40-55, 128-146`).

**crypto-world-fair:**
- `RangeReserve.openRange` takes side, low and high freely (`contracts/src/products/range/RangeReserve.sol:86-97`); settles inside/outside inclusive (`:144-157`).
- Same Moonshot rungs (`packages/core/src/range/moonshot.ts:19`).
- The terminal has mode chips Up/Down · Range · Boost · Private · Parlay, with Moonshot as a link to its own page (`web/src/features/terminal/ui/ModePanel.tsx:2-5, 20-31`).
- **The band is drawn on the chart as two levels labelled "Band ↑" and "Band ↓"** (`TerminalScreen.tsx:166-170`, `copy.ts:100-102`).
- End-to-end test: Range on `/games/range`, Moonshot long ×2, terminal Range mode, then settle and claim (`web/e2e/range.ts:1-11`).

**Masayume:** the same family. `openRange(marketId, asset, side, low, high, maxPayout, maxStake)` with settle and claim only, no close (`contracts/src/range/RangeReserve.sol:79-96, 140-152, 178`); same Moonshot rungs (`packages/core/src/range/moonshot.ts:18`).

## 4. Cleanest S7 design

**Range and Moonshot need no contract change.** Range (index 2), Moonshot (3) and Crash (4) are already live on all 12 series, priced, settled and cash-out-able. S7 can ship them as client work:
- Generalise `quoteTick` to the full menu, keyed by band index.
- Change `open(side)` to `open(bandIndex, label)`.
- Give the overlay a "between" zone for Range and a strike level for Moonshot, and work out "winning" with `bandOutcome(band, K, spot)`.
- Add level kinds for the band edges and the strike, drawn the way crypto-world-fair does.
- Fix the reaction side/line and the crowd split.
- Show edges as K ± `offsetE8`, which is only known once K is.
- Present Moonshot as a direction (▲ Moonshot = index 3, ▼ Crash = index 4).

**Optional extra rungs, still no contract change:** up to 3 more `addBand` entries per series (indices 5–7), e.g. Moonshot and Crash at about 1.4σ (≈10×) plus a tight Range at ±0.25σ (≈4.6×). This needs:
- A new script to append bands to the live deployment. `DeployMarkets` can't do it because `registerSeries` reverts if the series exists.
- Matching edits to `bandMenu` and `BAND_INDEX` in the catalog and to `SIDE`, since the API serves the config menu, not the chain menu.

**What does need a redeploy (the contract is a plain deployment, `DeployMarkets.s.sol:130`):**
1. Owarine-style free-form ranges (any centre and width) or a strike solved per quote: the menu is fixed and named by index.
2. Owarine's "outside" side: there is no such kind.
3. A range that doesn't contain K: `isValid` requires both offsets above 0 (`BandMath.sol:95`).
4. More than 8 bands per series (`MAX_BANDS`).

**Changing the parameters, not the contract:** the 25× rung is impossible under today's 3% floor plus 2 pp spread. `setParams` could lower the floor to as little as 1% (about 33×), but that bumps `configVersion`.

## Undefined, contradictory or dead ends

- **Staging contradicts itself.** The plan puts "Range and Moonshot menus" in S7 (`docs/plan/pivot-2026-10-08.md:398`). The parity table sends L-36 Range to "S8 games" (`docs/plan/parity-predictions.md:47`) and the native `/games/range` and `/games/moonshot` routes to S8 (`:183-184`).
- **Crash's role is undefined.** It is live on chain and in `SIDE`, but D-263 lists only Up, Down, Range and Moonshot (`pivot-2026-10-08.md:105-109`, `decisions.md:353`). Whether it surfaces as "Moonshot ▼" is not decided.
- **Moonshot means different things.** Owarine: you pick the multiple, the strike floats, and a close on the strike wins. Senryo: a fixed 1σ strike, the multiple floats over the window, and a close on the strike loses. The copy needs to follow Senryo.
- **Range differs too.** Owarine's is free-form with an outside side and no cash-out. Senryo's is fixed ±0.5σ around K, inside only, and can be cashed out. Owarine is no reference for cash-out on these bands.
- **Late in a window both bands go unpriced.** A Moonshot under 3% is refused for both open and cash-out, so it shows "Not priced now". A Range near K close to expiry goes over 97% and is refused the same way.
- **The API can drift from chain.** The catalog comes from config, not `menuOf`, so an on-chain `addBand` without a config change makes the API wrong.
- **The per-expiry cap and surcharge are shared across markets** that expire at the same instant. A Moonshot reserves about 4.6× its stake, against 0.92× for Up/Down.
- **25× can't be offered** under the current parameters.
