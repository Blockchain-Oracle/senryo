## 1. Owarine, game by game

Owarine's game list is `practice | duel | lucky | range | moonshot | line-rider | candle-hop`. Each game is tagged with whose money is at risk (none, a market order, a market order plus a side-pot, or a house position) — `packages/core/src/games/types.ts:15-46`. **Parlay is not one of the games**: it is a separate `/parlay` ticket (`web/src/features/parlay/*`, `mobile/src/features/parlay/*`).

### Duel — needs another player (for the side-pot) plus the venue (for each card)
- **The loop:**
  - Pick Free or Ranked. The side-pot is 0, 1, 5 or 10, with a cap of 1 per card (`types.ts:81-86`).
  - Queue for an opponent. The rating band starts at ±100, widens by 50 every 15 s, and stops at 400 (`matchmaking.ts:13-16`).
  - Both client seeds are revealed. The server deals the deck and publishes a keccak hash over chainId, arena, matchId, policyVersion, serverSeed, |clientSeeds|, clientSeeds, |cards|, cards (`core/games/commitment.ts:47-83`).
  - After the reveal, each swipe is a real order of at most the card cap. The last pick locks the match.
  - Each card settles on its own Window. The higher total of payout minus cost takes both pots. A tie splits, with the odd unit to the creator. One absent player forfeits; if both are absent, both are refunded (`scoring.ts:52-90`, `daml/abu-pm-games/daml/PM/Games/Arena.daml:344-404`).
- **The deck:** 2–5 distinct Windows, 15m first, 1h as fallback, mixed allowed, never 5m, at least 600 s left on each (`deck.ts:1-53`).
- **Ratings:** Elo starting at 1000; K is 48 for the first 10 verified matches, then 24 (`rating.ts:11-14`). There is also a season pool (`Season.daml`).
- **Copy:** "Market picks plus side-pot" (`types.ts:40`). The entry, queue, lobby, picking and result text is in `web/src/features/games/duel/copy.ts:62-260`, including "No network fee: the venue submits every ledger write" (:77) and auto-play at a card's deadline (:197).
- **Screens** (web `duel/` and `mobile/src/features/games/duel/`): Entry, Queue, Lobby ("Sealing the deck…"), Waiting, Picking (swipe deck), Result with share modal, Public result, Rank, History.
- **Settlement:**
  - The Daml flow is `ArenaTerms→DuelOpen→DuelMatch→DuelResult`. Picks are ordinary quote accepts tagged `duel:<arena>:<match>`, recorded with `Duel_RecordPick` and scored with `Duel_Score` (`Arena.daml:1-43,316-404`).
  - The Daml deck hash is **sha256** over length-prefixed text (`Deck.daml:28-45`), not keccak.
  - The supporting services (game room, matchmaker/deckmaster/seal, duel-settler, duel-projector, arena-desk) are in `services/ops/src/actors/*`.

### Practice — no counterparty (the opponent is a coin flip)
- Up to 5 live Windows are dealt as cards, each with at least 120 s left. The entry price is the live spot at the swipe.
- After the last swipe there is a 30 s watch. Every card is then scored at the same instant **on the live feed, not on the Window's settlement**. A flat price is nobody's card.
- The bot's side is the top bit of an FNV-1a hash of seed:index (`practice.ts:21-40,109-135,210-236`).
- Copy: "No stake · no position · nothing on the ledger" (`practice/copy.ts:12-19`). Screens: Stage, Row, Watch, Result.
- It runs entirely in the client.

### Lucky — the venue is the counterparty (one ordinary call)
- **The draw:**
  - Set a stake and spin. The server commits keccak(serverSeed) (`lucky.server.ts:57-66`), and the browser then adds its own seed.
  - HMAC-SHA256 keyed by the server seed, over clientSeed|wallet|nonce|policy (`lucky-digest.server.ts:16-18`), is turned into unbiased indexes for asset, side and reach. The reach is one of {2,3,5,10,25}× (`commitment.ts:105-144`, `lucky.ts:52`).
- **The order:**
  - Among Windows with at least 120 s left (5m excluded), it takes the fully fillable quote closest to 1/M; on a tie, the soonest expiry wins (`lucky.ts:87-133`).
  - The card shows the seed proof and the live quote, and says so if the price drifted more than 10% (:58,136). One tap places one real order.
- **Results:** the streak counts verified wins and skips voids (:158-186). Copy is in `lucky/copy.ts:12-53`. Rate limit is 120 spins per hour per address (`lucky.server.ts:35-36`). There is no Daml for Lucky.

### Parlay — the house reserve is the counterparty
- **Building a ticket:**
  - The UI allows 2–3 legs (`parlay/pricing.ts:8-9`); the Daml allows 2–4 (`Parlay.daml:36-38`). Each leg is a Window plus Up or Down, with the opening print as the line.
  - Two modes: fix the stake or fix the payout (`types.ts:92`).
- **Pricing:** multiply the leg prices together. If two legs share an expiry, the result is floored at λ × the lowest leg (a correlation adjustment). The minimum stake is fair value × (1 + margin). Tickets are refused for a thin book, a long shot, going over the cap, or being underpriced (`pricing.ts:28-156`).
- **Settlement:**
  - The full maximum payout is escrowed at open, and legs are decided in the order they close.
  - A lost leg means the reserve keeps the money. A void leg refunds the stake. The last winning leg pays out (`Parlay.daml:1-21,160-183`).
- Copy: "Odds multiply / All-or-nothing / Pre-funded payout", and "Two coin flips already pay roughly 4×" (`parlay/copy.ts:15-31`).

### Line Rider and Candle Hop — no money, no counterparty
- Line Rider: keep the dot on a line to build a combo; drifting drains grip. Candle Hop: tap to flap through candle gaps (`games/copy.ts:77-78`).
- The line and the candles come from **seeded random numbers, not prices** (`ride.ts:112-118`, `flap.ts:116`). The engine steps at a fixed 60 Hz, and a run is at most 30 minutes (`field.ts:15-23`).
- The server replays the seed and the recorded inputs, and only records a score it reproduces exactly. It keeps a top-10 board and labels scores "server-checked · not on-chain" (`arcade/score.server.ts:1-60`).

- **Interface:** `IGameArena.sol:13-174` defines the status and refund enums, tiers, params, Match, PickRecord, Agent, events and errors.
- **Commitment:** `ArenaCommitment.sol:13-45` builds the keccak preimage. A golden test vector shared with the TypeScript side is in `test/ArenaVectors.t.sol:35-80`.
- **`GameArena.sol`:**
  - `createMatch` / `createMatchWithAgent` names the challenger and escrows the pot (:39-95).
  - `joinMatch` (:98-125).
  - `revealDeck` checks the hash, that each card has at least `minCardLifeSec` left, and no duplicates (:133-161).
  - `placePick` / `placePickFor` place an immediate-or-cancel order on DreamDEX, capped per card, refund the unspent stake, and lock the match on the last pick (:169-237).
  - `lockPicks` handles forfeit and refund (:242-259).
  - `settleCard` redeems both seats and credits the profit or loss (:265-293).
  - `finalize` pays the pot (:298-327).
- **Helpers:**
  - `ArenaMatches.sol:37-73`: cancel, refund-unjoined, refund-unrevealed, and `claimCredit`, which pays the player and never the caller.
  - `ArenaAgents.sol:30-73`: a key scoped to one match, with a budget of cap × deck size, and gas forwarded through `msg.value`.
  - The DreamDEX connection is `ArenaGateway.sol:124-234`.
- **Deployed settings:** join 180 s, reveal 120 s, pick 180 s, deck 3–5 cards, card life 240 s, tiers 0/1/5/10 (`script/DeployGameArena.s.sol:30-44`).
- **Tests:** 46 (lifecycle 14, picks 21, agents 7, fork 1, vectors 3). `SeasonPrizePool.sol:61-107` has its own test.

### Parlay — Mitoshi `ParlayReserve` (MI `src/products/parlay/`, ported from Masayume's)
- **Maths:** `ParlayMath.sol` has the volume-weighted book price, the combine rule and the stake floor (:23-63).
- **Pricing:**
  - `ParlayPricing._price` (:124-156) needs 2 to `maxLegs` distinct legs, all trading and before their lock time.
  - Each leg is priced from the order book over a depth of at least the larger of the payout and the minimum depth. There is also a long-shot floor, and the stake must be below the payout.
  - `_checkFair` bounds each leg against a fair-value model on a signed spot price (:159-166).
- **Reserve:**
  - It has its own supplier pool with `supply` and `withdraw` (:56-85).
  - `openParlay` escrows the stake plus the house's share, and locks it against each distinct expiry (:88-139, line 105).
  - Caps cover liquidity, total exposure and per-expiry exposure (:255-266).
  - `resolveLeg` is permissionless and decides legs in close order (won / lost / void), releasing each expiry's lock as it goes (:153-189, 282-323).
  - `claim` pays the owner (:192-201).
- **Tests:** 43, including a fork test (`test/products/parlay/*`, `test/fork/Moderato.ParlayReserve.fork.t.sol`). Masayume's original has no fair-value check (MA `src/parlay/*`, `test/Parlay*.t.sol`).

## 3. The cleanest design on Senryo's contracts

**What Senryo already has:**
- There is no order book. A signed intent is committed, then filled at the print one second later.
  - Price per share = band probability (normal model using σ and time left) + half-spread + surcharge.
  - Payout = stake ÷ price (`BandBook.sol:62-111,197-227`, `BandMath.sol:107-149`).
- Trading stops 20 s before expiry (`MarketTypes.sol:52`).
- `settleWindow` applies each band's outcome; Up and Down are refunded when the close equals the open (`BandReserve.sol:62-98`, `BandMath.sol:127-144`).
- The pool's books are internal to BandPool (`BandPool.sol:113-157`).
  - Earn (PoolShares) values **only** BandReserve (`PoolShares.sol:7-15`).
  - Its hourly roll waits until every expiry in the past hour has nothing reserved (:225-238).
- Intents accept contract signatures (ERC-1271) for the owner (`SessionGrants.sol:126-147`).
- Window lengths are 1m, 5m, 15m and 1h.
- BandReserve is 26,231 bytes deployed. That is over the usual Ethereum limit, but fine under Monad's 128 KB (`foundry.toml:10`).
- No games code exists yet, only parity rows L-61 to L-71 (`SN/docs/plan/parity-predictions.md:72-82`).

**Who is on the other side:**

| Game | Counterparty | Needs a contract? |
|---|---|---|
| Duel | Other player for the pot; the pool for each card | Yes — new `DuelArena.sol` |
| Parlay | The shared pool | Yes — a module inside BandReserve |
| Lucky | The shared pool (one ordinary call) | No |
| Practice | A coin-flip bot, no money | No — client only |
| Line Rider, Candle Hop | None (leaderboard only) | No — client plus one api route |

### Duel — a new `DuelArena.sol`
- **Port as-is:** Masayume's interface, match/refund/credit logic, keccak commitment, and the GameArena lifecycle. Cards become window ids (bytes32) in the commitment.
- **Replace the DreamDEX connection:**
  - The arena itself owns each pick's ticket. It approves the digest in the same transaction (ERC-1271).
  - For a pick, the arena pulls at most the card cap from the player. Authority comes from a signed `DuelPick` or a match-scoped delegate, without the `msg.value` gas funding. It then calls `BandReserve.commit` with an Up/Down intent whose recipient is the arena.
  - At reveal, check `Windows.windowOf` for enough life left and an open print.
- **Scoring:**
  - `settleCard` reads `windowSettled`, `ticketOf` and `bandOutcome`, then calls `claimFor`.
  - The player is credited the payout on a win, the stake on a refund, and the stake back if the pick was refused. Profit or loss is the sum of credits minus stakes.
  - The arena never cashes out a pick.
- **Entry:** users hold no gas, so entry is a signed `DuelEntry` plus a token permit, sent by the relay.
- **Off-chain work:**
  - A room and queue (the api's `stream` module).
  - A deckmaster that picks distinct windows with an open print and enough time left, plus the seed exchange.
  - Relay lanes for entries and picks.
  - Keeper jobs for refunds, `lockPicks`, `settleCard`, `finalize` and `claimCredit`.
  - Indexer support with Elo and the ladder, and a new migration `0018_games`.
- **Tests:** port the 46 tests and the golden vector.
- **UI:** hub, entry, queue, lobby, swipe picking, settling, result and share, rank, history, public match link.
- **Alternative:** a pot-only duel scored by number of correct calls, with picks hidden by commit-reveal. The catch is that waiting until the last second wins, because Up/Down is nearly decided just before the lockout.

### Parlay — a `ParlayBook` module inside BandReserve, paid by the shared pool
- **Why inside:** the pool's books are internal, and Earn only values BandReserve. A separate ParlayReserve would have its own pool and bypass Earn.
- **Opening and filling:**
  - `commitParlay` takes a `ParlayIntent` (legs as window + band pairs, stake, minimum payout, and so on) with a signature and permit.
  - `finalizeParlay(target, ids, proofs[])` records a print for each distinct price feed, then prices each leg with `BandMath.probE6`. It multiplies them, applying Mitoshi's correlation floor, and adds the margin.
  - It refuses two legs on the same window or overlapping windows of the same series, and refuses long shots. It then checks capacity.
- **Reserving:**
  - Count the house's share once in `reserved`.
  - Add it to `reservedByExpiry` for each distinct expiry (Mitoshi line 105), so Earn's roll waits.
- **Resolving and claiming:** `resolveLeg` uses the Window's verdict plus `BandMath.outcome` on the band menu, in close order. `claim` and `expire` complete the ticket.
- **Tests:** port Mitoshi's close-order tests (D-108), per-expiry release tests (L-38), and the maths vectors.
- **Off-chain:**
  - A relay lane and fills that span several feeds.
  - The keeper's settle job must include parlay-leg windows (`SN/services/keeper/src/jobs/settle.ts:22` only picks windows that have tickets), plus `resolveLeg` cranks and automatic payouts.
- **UI:** builder (2–3 legs, a streak preset), a slip showing the multiplier and combined chance, and tickets whose legs tick green as they settle.

### Lucky — no contract
- **Api:** port `lucky.ts`, `commitment.ts` and the HMAC digest. Map the draw to (market, band, reach).
- **Choosing the call:** pick the window and band whose `BandReserve.quoteOpen` multiple is closest to the drawn reach.
- **Placing it:** return the proof and the quote; a tap is an ordinary open intent through the session.
- **Storage:** a draws table; streaks come from settled tickets.

### Practice and arcade — no chain
- **Practice:** the live window list plus the price stream, with `practice.ts` unchanged.
- **Arcade:**
  - Port `core/games/arcade` (pure TypeScript) with canvas fields on web and Skia on mobile.
  - Add an api score route: replay, rate limit, store in an `arcade_scores` table, serve the board.

## UNDEFINED / contradictory / dead-end

**Practice**
1. Owarine contradicts itself. The hub's how-to says Practice scores "on the real closing prints" and ends "when the last card settles" (`web/src/features/games/copy.ts:72`). The engine and the stage copy score on the live feed 30 s after the last swipe (`practice.ts:8-22`, `practice/copy.ts:18`).
2. Name clash: in Senryo, "Practice" is already the test-dollar mode with $1,000 per call and $10,000 per session (`SN/docs/plan/pivot-2026-10-08.md:6,131,322`).

**Duel**
3. What "keccak commit-reveal" commits to is undefined: the deck (Owarine core and Masayume) or the picks. Owarine's Daml uses sha256 (`Deck.daml:43-45`).
4. Duel economics are undefined. Owarine uses real orders plus a side-pot; the S8 plan just says "escrowed head-to-head".
5. Minimum deck size conflicts: 2 in Owarine core (`deck.ts:24-39`), 3 in Masayume's deploy script (`DeployGameArena.s.sol:33`), and "three to five" in the docs.

**Parlay**
6. Maximum legs conflicts: 3 in the UI, 4 in Daml, a parameter in Mitoshi.
7. A leg tied exactly at the open (Senryo refunds those) could void the whole ticket (the Mitoshi/Owarine rule) or just drop the leg. Not decided.
8. Owarine's "close streak" (consecutive future windows) can't be built yet: a commit needs the window's open print (`BandBook.sol:80`). Pricing a window that hasn't opened is not designed.
9. Parlay is a ticket, not a game, in Owarine (`types.ts:15`). Range and Moonshot, which are Owarine games, already shipped as Senryo S7 bands.

**Lucky**
10. Lucky's 25× reach can't be reached on Senryo. The lowest price is the 3% probability floor plus the 2% spread, so the maximum is 20× (`SN/packages/config/src/pool-terms.ts:94-99`, `BandBook.sol:200-206`).

**Operations**
11. The keeper only settles windows that have BandReserve tickets (`settle.ts:22`), so parlay-leg windows must be added.
12. Masayume's SeasonPrizePool would pay real money on mainnet, but the plan only mentions test-dollar referral bonuses. Not decided.

**Dead-ends (don't port)**

14. DreamDEX order placement and outcome-token redemption (`ArenaGateway.sol:165-189`), and gas funding through `msg.value` (`ArenaAgents.sol:68-73`).
