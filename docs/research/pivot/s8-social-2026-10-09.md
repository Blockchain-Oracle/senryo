# S8 research — social, streaks, referral, ticker, Notify me (9 Oct 2026, read-only agent)

I couldn't check two things. Mitoshi has no social screens built: its leaderboard and profile pages are placeholders (`crypto-world-fair/web/src/app/(app)/leaderboard/page.tsx:1-9`, `web/src/lib/capabilities.ts:53-56`). And neither reference has a referral or a server-side "notify me" to compare against. Everything below was read only; nothing was changed.

## 1. What Senryo has today

**Handles and profiles: built in the api, half-built in the apps.**
- api: `GET /v1/handles/:h/available`, `GET` and `PUT /v1/profile`, `GET /v1/profile/:handleOrAddress?chainId`, `DELETE /v1/social` (`services/api/src/routes/profile.ts:20-52`). Handle locks, a 30-day hold on released handles and a limit on renames are in `services/api/src/social/profiles.ts:65-176`. An unlisted profile answers 404, same as no profile (`profiles.ts:183-204`).
- Visibility: four switches, each network listed and "show my calls" separately (`packages/api-client/src/routes/profile.ts:51-60`). Defaults are Practice on, Real off (`packages/api-client/src/handles.ts:46-51`). The `*_since` columns mean a call made before sharing was switched on never shows (`profiles.ts:140-155`).
- Phone: Edit profile with visibility settings (`apps/mobile/src/app/account/profile.tsx`, `features/profile/ProfileEditor.tsx:184`), the More tab header (`(tabs)/more/index.tsx:71`), and the handle step in setup (`app/setup/handle.tsx:42,88`). The header shows only "joined"; its comment says "Calls and win rate join in S8" (`ProfileHeader.tsx:166-178`).
- Web: only the handle step in setup (`apps/web/src/features/setup/HandleStep.tsx:49`). No profile editing, no visibility settings, no profile in the nav (`packages/config/src/nav.ts:82-226`).
- Missing on both apps: a page showing someone else's profile. `useProfile` is only used for your own profile (`useOwnProfile.ts:56`, `SignInOutcome.tsx:31`, `SubjectMark.tsx:43`). `PersonRow.tsx` is never used.

**Leaderboard: api only.**
- `reader.leaderboard(chainId, "day" | "week" | "all")` returns the top 50 rows of owner, handle, pnl, calls and wins (`services/api/src/history/reader.ts:159-177`). Route: `GET /v1/markets/leaderboard` (`routes/history.ts:91-106`). Hook: `useLeaderboard` (`packages/query/src/history.ts:164`).
- Practice and Real are kept apart by `chainId` on every query.
- Day and week use UTC calendar days, not rolling windows (`reader.ts:169-170`).
- Accounts with no listed profile still appear, by address, with a null handle (`reader.ts:164`).
- No avatar or display name in rows, no "your rank", no app screen. The nav says the leaderboard arrives at S8 (`nav.ts:53`).

**Caller stats and streaks: indexer and api done; the apps show only the best streak.**
- `Account` stores `streak` and `bestStreak` (`indexer/schema.graphql:102-117`).
- Rule: a win adds one, a loss resets to zero, a refund leaves it unchanged (`indexer/src/lib/records.ts:70-81`). A cash-out counts as a win only if proceeds are above the stake, so a break-even cash-out is a loss (`indexer/src/handlers/tickets.ts:216`).
- Route: `GET /v1/markets/stats` (`routes/history.ts:108-123`).
- Both apps show "best streak N" on Calls (web `CallsScreen.tsx:58`, mobile `CallsScreen.tsx:72`). The current streak is never shown.

**Share card: both apps, finished calls only.**
- `shareOf` builds the card "for a finished call" (`packages/calls/src/receipt.ts:110-129`).
- Web draws it on a canvas at 1080×1350 (`apps/web/src/features/calls/share-card.ts`); the phone captures a view and opens the share sheet (`apps/mobile/src/features/calls/ShareCallButton.tsx`).
- The button is hidden while a call is live (mobile `CallReceipt.tsx:93-95`, web `CallReceipt.tsx:90-98`).
- The link goes to `/call?id&chainId` (`packages/calls/src/links.ts:7-10`). The card carries no handle and no referral code.

**Crowd split: both apps, Up and Down only.**
- `CrowdLine` on the trade screen of each app shows only bands 0 and 1 (web `features/terminal/CrowdLine.tsx:14-29`, mobile `CrowdLine.tsx:16-34`).
- `crowdText` covers every band and is used on the proof pages (`packages/calls/src/receipt.ts:144-156`, used at `:207` and in `apps/web/src/features/proof/WindowPage.tsx:131`).
- So there are two separate implementations. The indexer adds to `bandStake` when a call fills and never subtracts on cash-out (`tickets.ts:131`).

**Recent-calls ticker: none.**
- The stream lists `markets` as a public topic for "public call activity" (`services/api/src/stream/route.ts:10,14`), but nothing sends on it and no client subscribes (`packages/live/src/live.ts:12` only takes `prices` and `prints`).
- The profile schema already promises calls will appear "in the recent-calls ticker" (`api-client/src/routes/profile.ts:88`).

**Referral: none.** Only the reserved handle "referral" (`social/constants.ts:100`) and wording in the notification settings (`apps/mobile/src/app/account/notifications.tsx:35-37`, `PushHost.tsx:23`).

**Push and notifications: the plumbing is built; only call results use it.**
- The `push_sends` outbox, an idempotent record function and the "Practice ·" title prefix are in `services/common/src/notifications.ts:57-77`.
- Delivery through Expo (`push-delivery.ts`), the keeper's push and receipt jobs (`keeper/src/jobs/pushes.ts`, `receipts.ts`), and the notifier (`keeper/src/notify.ts:32-59`).
- Channels are results, deposits, priceAlerts and social (`api-client/src/routes/engagement.ts:30-47`), stored as `ch_*` columns (`routes/engagement.ts:53-71`).
- The only thing that sends a push today is the win/refund result (`keeper/src/results.ts:24-47`). `notifyArrival` is never called (`notifications.ts:114`). Nothing sends on social or priceAlerts.
- Phone: inbox, bell, Android channels and per-channel switches (`features/notifications/*`, `lib/notifications/PushHost.tsx:19-24`). Web: no inbox, no service worker, no push.

**Stream user topic: works.** It carries ticket, intent, session, dollars and earn events (`services/api/src/main.ts:81-88`, `relay/accounts.ts:61-142`, `packages/live/src/live.ts:10-11`).

**Notify me when a market opens: none.**
- The "closed" panel on both apps has no button (web `CallPanel.tsx:91-99`, mobile `CallPanel.tsx:77-87`).
- The `price_alerts` table was dropped in migration 0015 (`services/common/migrations/0015_prediction_pivot.ts:12`).
- `sessionNow().nextSec` already gives the next open time (`packages/core/src/market/session/words.ts:12-48`).

## 3. S8 gap list and the smallest correct design

1. **Profiles on the web plus public profile pages.**
   - Port Edit profile and its visibility settings to a web drawer `?d=profile`.
   - Public page: web `/u?h=` (a query parameter, as `/call` does, since the web is a static export); phone `/u/[handle]`.
   - Content: identity, stats from `/v1/markets/stats`, and the person's public calls.
   - New api route: `GET /v1/profile/:h/calls?chainId`. It only returns calls if `public_trades_<net>` is on, and only calls since the `_since` time (`profiles.ts:140-155`).
   - Fill in the calls and win-rate line in `ProfileHeader.tsx:166`.
2. **Leaderboard.**
   - Change rows to carry handle, display name and avatar (`socialIdentitySchema`, `api-client/src/routes/profile.ts:146-152`).
   - Add an optional `owner` query that returns `me: {rank, row} | null`.
   - Screens: web `/app/leaderboard/` in the rail and Everything list; phone `/leaderboard` in `MORE_NAV`.
   - Layout: podium, then field, then a You bar. Practice and Real are already separated by `env.chainId`.
3. **Streaks.** Show the current streak (`stats.streak`) next to the best streak on Calls and on profiles. Add `bestStreak` to "all" rows from `Account`.
4. **Share card.** Add "by @handle" when the profile is listed on that network, and add `&r=<handle>` to `callLink` so the card also carries the referral.
5. **Referral.**
   - New table `referrals(referee PK, referrer, chain_id=testnet, created_at, rewarded_at, tx_hash)`.
   - The code is the referrer's handle, captured from `?r=` on the web welcome page and the phone deep link.
   - `POST /v1/referrals {code}` needs a session. Refused for yourself, for a second claim, and after the person's first call.
   - When the person's first call fills, the existing `TICKET_CHANNEL` listener (`main.ts:81`, change `filled`) has the api relay (`relay/accounts.ts:112-145`) mint the bonus to both accounts. It also records a `social` push.
   - Add the table to `delete-data.ts:31-39`.
6. **Crowd split.** Write one shared helper in `@senryo/calls` that gives each band's share. Use it in both apps' `CrowdLine` and in `crowdText`, so Range and Moonshot show on the trade screen.
7. **Recent-calls ticker.**
   - On a `filled` notice, look up the market and band from `market_tickets`, and the profile from `profiles` (`visibleOn`, `public_trades_<net>`).
   - Send it on the `markets` topic with `chainId` included.
   - First paint: `GET /v1/markets/recent?chainId`, read from the indexer's `Ticket` table.
   - Add `markets` to the client's public topics in `live.ts:12`; show the strip on Home and Markets in both apps.
8. **Notify me.**
   - New table `market_alerts(address, chain_id, symbol, open_at, sent_at, PK(address, chain_id, symbol))`.
   - `PUT`, `DELETE` and `GET /v1/alerts/market-open`, session-authed.
   - A keeper job, `opens`: for each alert whose `open_at` has passed and whose market is open now, record a `priceAlerts` notification with key `open:<sym>:<open_at>:<addr>`, title "<SYM> is open", link `trade/<sym>`, subject the market. The alert is used once.
   - Add a "Notify me" button to both closed panels. Add the table to `delete-data.ts`.

## UNDEFINED / contradictory / dead-end

- **Leaderboard privacy:** the leaderboard ranks unlisted accounts by address (`reader.ts:163-176`), and `/v1/markets/calls?owner=` ignores the "show my calls" setting (`routes/history.ts:24-28`). Both go against the privacy model described in `api-client/src/routes/profile.ts:12-16`. Owarine ranks only published calls. Needs a decision: show unlisted accounts anonymised, or leave them out.
- **Notify me on the web:** S7.3 said "a browser notification on the web" (`pivot-s7-markets.md:71-72`); the move to S8 says "a server-side alert and a push" (`:77`). The web has no push, no service worker and no inbox. Needs a decision: Web Push, or in-tab notifications only.
- **Price-alert copy with no feature behind it:** the settings promise "a price you set is crossed" (`apps/mobile/src/app/account/notifications.tsx:31-32`, `engagement.ts:35`). Price alerts were dropped (`0015:12`) and are not in the S8 list. Either build them or cut the copy.
- **"A stock market you watch":** the watchlist is stored encrypted, so the server can't read it (`packages/account/src/prefs.ts:1-27`). No screen uses it. "Notify me" needs its own explicit subscription.
- **Referral bonus vs. the daily top-up:** Practice tops the balance back up to $1,000 every day (`relay/accounts.ts:112-137`, `constants.ts:11-12`). A bonus inside that cap is erased by the next top-up. Needs a rule, for example that the bonus sits above the cap or raises it.
- **Follows:** promised in `packages/query/src/profiles.ts:2` and in parity row A-3a (`parity-predictions.md:110`), but not in the S8 scope (`pivot-2026-10-08.md:404`).
- **Streak edges:** a break-even cash-out counts as a loss and resets the streak. Day and week boards count calls on the day they fill but PnL on the day they finish (`tickets.ts:146` vs `:53`).
- **Halt watch missing:** the S7.3 checkbox lists "the halt watch" and "Trading halted", but no halt code exists anywhere in `services/` or `packages/core`. A "market open" push could fire during a halt.
- **Dead code:**
  - Old perp-era social constants nothing uses (`services/api/src/social/constants.ts:163-278`).
  - `notifyArrival` (`notifications.ts:114`) and `PersonRow.tsx`, both never used.
  - The `markets` stream topic, with no sender and no subscriber.
  - Out-of-date comments at `api-client/src/routes/engagement.ts:5` and `services/api/src/routes/engagement.ts:18`.
  - The "The Call" card for a placed call (parity L-22) was never built.
