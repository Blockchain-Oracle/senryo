# Public market activity — 7 October 2026

The Global social feed and market Feed tabs previously contained only Senryo fills from traders who opted in to public sharing. That is the right source for named traders and theses, but it is often empty. The mobile app now places a separate **Market activity** section before **Senryo community**. It reads recent public Binance spot executions on the focused screen, refreshes every 15 seconds, names the exact pair and venue, and shows taker direction, base quantity, approximate USDT notional and source age. It does not attach a person, wallet, Senryo position or P&L to an outside trade.

Source: [Binance official Spot REST documentation](https://github.com/binance/binance-spot-api-docs/blob/master/rest-api.md), including its public market-data host, `aggTrades` response and `m` buyer-maker field; [Spot glossary](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/spot/faqs/spot_glossary) for aggregate-trade semantics. `m=true` means the resting order was a buy, so the taker sold. The parser rejects malformed, non-positive, future and older-than-one-hour executions. A query failure remains labelled unavailable or shows the last verified rows with their actual ages. Requests stop when the route loses focus.

Verified live pairs this session: BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, ZEC/USDT, SOL/USDT, NEAR/USDT, PUMP/USDT, EUR/USDT and PAXG/USDT. Gold/XAU links to **PAXG/USDT**, explicitly labelled as gold-backed token spot trading, not a Senryo XAU/USD fill or its executable price. Global shows BTC, ETH and PAXG. Unsupported or stale pairs do not receive substitute rows; XAG, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, MON and LIT currently have no verified current outside tape. The Senryo community sections still preserve network and privacy boundaries.

Acceptance: live PAXG response parsed to 24 positive executions; synthetic buyer-maker and stale cases checked; mobile TypeScript, focused Biome and repository invariants passed.
