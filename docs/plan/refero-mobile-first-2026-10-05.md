# Refero: mobile-first product correction

The user's explicit correction is the product authority: Senryo is a **mobile app for accessible pair trading on Monad, predictions, money flows and cards**. Social helps people find traders and understand their activity. A browser experience exists as a companion; it must not lead the landing-page conversion. Meme-coin discovery is not the product positioning. Existing asset compatibility is preserved rather than indiscriminately deleted.

The prior website prototype was not published. Keep its owned Senryo seal, card and approved art; correct the product framing and introduce actual mobile-screen evidence. Do not import other companies' screenshots into public Senryo assets.
