## Decisions and screen contract

- Five destinations: Home, Markets, Card, Social, **Profile**, plus the right-side action fan. Withdraw and Activity are Home money actions; pool holdings belong to investments.
- Home leads with estimated total portfolio, then Add money/Withdraw, trading/card availability, positions, holdings and pool investment. No trading curve or trading return is labelled total portfolio performance.
- Mode selection uses authentic Monad identity, two compact Practice/Mainnet rows, balance and selected check. Mainnet entry keeps deliberate confirmation and session locking.
- Funding: Mainnet defaults to wallet receive/send; Practice defaults to trading-account funding. Wallet address and deposit inbox are distinct, labelled destinations. Wallet → trading is an explicit transfer. Each method keeps its own unavailable reason and Back.
- Send: recipient/address or eligible handle → exact amount → review → slide/authentication → result. AUSD is the asset, not AUD. Self-withdrawal goes to the wallet, not an implied bank account.
- Markets retain priced discovery even when execution is unavailable. Following replaces the user-facing Friends label. Detail leads with identity, price/chart, compact metrics and anchored Long/Short; sources/rules belong in About.
- Ticket: prominent amount, side/leverage, concise exposure/fee/liquidation, optional TP/SL and keypad/chart switch. Preparing gas is a brief progress state; technical lifecycle details remain secondary.
- Positions show PnL/entry/current/protection and Reduce/Close. A successful open with failed protection remains an open position with unfinished protection.
- Kinpaku uses restrained artwork, actual service state, spending availability, controls and real activity. No invented holders/numbers/merchants. Unissued and unavailable-service states differ. Gray visual refinements wait for evidence.
- Profile leads with identity/follows, period-based trading performance and positions/activity. Share/history/Settings are header controls. Browsing Settings never authenticates; sensitive changes retain step-up.
- Pool: investment including escrowed requests, pending redemptions, concise metrics, exact Deposit/Redeem/Max. Capital risk, 24-hour delay, open-market requirement and variable value remain visible before approval.

## Shared implementation contract

Installation welcome, account setup and authentication are independent. Restored Keychain hints do not bypass unseen welcome; deliberate completed welcome survives updates. Pending links wait through welcome/account setup. Replay welcome is a preference.

No automatic unfinished send/protection after relaunch. Revalidate after gas/authentication and before broadcast. Signed uncertain results remain pending; age alone never authorizes a replacement. Portfolio snapshots use one finalized block for wallet, inbox, trading account and active/escrowed pool holdings. Exposure, margin and card capacity are not added. Unknown values show Partial with missing sources. Perpl equity joins only when its account integration is operational.

Original bundled short quiet cues are default on, independent of haptics, respect silent mode, mix with other audio and never play in the background. Settled welcome scenes, welcome completion and live finalized trade/transfer outcomes may cue. Navigation, loading, cancelled authentication and approval transactions stay silent. Central outcome ownership deduplicates by operation outcome; historical recovery remains visible without a sound queue.

Card/recovery secrets require authenticated reveal, one shared capture-protection owner, awaited protection and hide on background. Masked card artwork remains shareable. Card summary extends the existing typed service; profile feed adds actor pagination; pool requests query all indexed requests for the account/chain and verify onchain.
