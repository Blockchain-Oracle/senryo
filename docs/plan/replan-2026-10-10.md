## Defaults I chose (say if you want any changed)

1. **Show PnL the Tradash way.**
   - It starts at $0.00 and is counted from the fair price when your call filled.
   - The spread shows as a Fees row, and a break-even tag sits on the chart.
   - The Cash out button always shows the exact amount you'd get.
2. **Real-money rides on the web only for now. Practice rides on both apps.** Apple's derivatives-licensing rule (3.2.2(viii)) puts phone review at risk. It's one switch at S9.
3. **Mainnet starts with Up and Down.** Range, Moonshot and Crash join once their pricing passes a check on Pyth's own history. A listed band can never be removed.
4. **Earn suppliers back both calls and rides,** with rides capped at 30% of the pool. This is the one-pool pattern of D-260 and D-293.
5. **No arrow-key or C-key trading with real money** unless one-tap is on. Practice keeps the hotkeys.
6. **Web is light by default,** as on the phone. Dark follows UGLYCASH, never the old purple. The pink takes a black label: about 6.5:1 contrast, against 3.2:1 for white.
7. **The half-finished markets deploy is abandoned.** About 7.6 MON is already spent. v3 deploys once, fresh, against the parts that survive (Windows, the five verifiers, the calendar, TestUSD, AccessManager).

## Pricing decisions (my call, as asked)

### Up/Down calls (contract v3)

- **Volatility that follows the market.**
  - A keeper-signed σ mark per series: a 30-minute EWMA, refreshed each minute, carried in `finalize`'s calldata.
  - The contract checks the signer, freshness (≤ 2 min) and a floor and cap per series. No global version bump, so nothing in flight is refused.
- **A calibrated fat-tailed probability table** replaces the normal Φ table (`BandMath.sol`). It's fitted on Pyth prints from the archive, with Binance's 90 days as the proxy until there's enough.
- **Band widths** come from realised σ; the ×1.5 goes.
- **The spread follows Polymarket's and Kalshi's curve:** h(p) = max(0.25 pt, 4·h50·p(1−p)) per series. h50 by lane:
  - 1m 3.0 (Practice only);
  - 5m 2.0;
  - 15m 1.5;
  - 1h 1.0.

  So Up opens at −7.7%, −5.8% and −3.9% (5m, 15m, 1h), and Moonshot at about −13% instead of −22%. Keep the 1-point load surcharge. **Not 1 point flat:** at 1 point a 3-second momentum bot already wins on 1-minute calls.
- **`FILL_DELAY_SEC` becomes a bounded setting (1–3 s).** It moves to 2 s if Pyth's lag behind the exchanges (measured in R1) is above about 0.5 s.
- **The slippage limit is set in price terms:** the 99th percentile of a 2-second move (BTC 3, ETH 4.5, SOL 5.5 bp). It replaces 3% of the quote, so refusals fall from 7–15% to about 1%.

### Ride the price (LinearBook)

- **A position:**
  - each ride is isolated;
  - UP is long, DOWN is short;
  - it fills at the next Pyth print;
  - it is knocked out at 90% of margin lost;
  - its maximum profit is reserved at the fill;
  - it lives 4 hours at most and settles at that print;
  - no funding.
- **Fees:**
  - 5 bps a side on BTC/ETH/SOL, 8 on other crypto, 10 on RedStone and stocks;
  - 2 bps when the trade reduces the pool's skew;
  - an early-close fee on profitable closes, falling from 20 to 0 bps over 15 s;
  - no extra spread.
- **Leverage:** testnet 40× BTC, 25× ETH, 20× SOL, 10× others (Tradash/Bulk). Mainnet 20× / 10× / 5×.
- **Profit cap:** testnet margin × min(900%, L × 25%); mainnet min(200%, L × 10%).
- **Pool limits:**
  - rides use at most 30% of the pool;
  - a reserve cap per market;
  - a skew cap that always lets the reducing side trade;
  - at most 400 open per side;
  - an hourly loss brake (the Ostium lesson).
- **v1 markets:** the 24/7 Pyth crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, BNB, DOGE, HYPE).

## How we work
- **No worktrees.** One checkout, stages in order, and others' files stashed out of builds.
- **Components:** 21st.dev is searched first for every component, with the reference file as the behaviour spec and provenance in `.21st/design.json`.
- **Design direction:** questions go to the Codex CLI (xhigh); only scope and money choices come to you.
- **Code:** constants rather than magic numbers, files ≤ 400 lines, and one implementation per capability (superseded paths deleted in the same stage).
- **Tradash itself:** behaviour and numbers only — never its code, images or MP3s (it has no licence).
