## Eligibility (§2)
- **Age 18+** as of 1 Sep 2026 (under 18 only with verifiable parent/guardian consent).
- Worldwide, **except sanctioned jurisdictions** (US OFAC, EU, UN). Registration details and **wallet addresses may be screened**.
- Employees/contractors of the organizer, sponsors and judges (and immediate family) **can't win prizes**.
- **Teams of 1–5.** Every member registers; one member is the primary contact, and prize money goes to them.
- **One project per participant.** Each submission competes in **one track only**, and can't win multiple main-track prizes.

## Mandatory submission requirements (§4.1)
1. **Public GitHub repository**: complete source, README with setup instructions, **open source license (MIT, Apache 2.0, GPL or similar)**, attribution of external code, and **a commit history covering the build window**. Track pages add: *"fully accessible by metropolis@hackathon.monad.xyz."* §7.2: *"Code must be publicly accessible on GitHub throughout and after the Hackathon."*
2. **Demo video (3 min)**, public (YouTube/Loom/Vimeo), showing the product working and the Monad integration.
3. **Monad integration**: explain how you use Monad, give contract addresses, and deploy to mainnet or testnet.
4. **Originality**: *"The substantial majority of the work"* must be built during the hackathon. Pre-existing code is allowed only if identified in the README and substantial new work is added. **AI coding tool use must be disclosed in the README.**
5. **Documentation**: project description, architecture overview, tech stack, setup/deployment instructions.
6. Submit through the hackathon website.

**Track pages add these deliverables** (the same for all four tracks): logo/graphic (JPG/PNG/WEBP, ≤3 MB); a **technical demo video ≤3 min** (*"must show the live working product, not slides or a code walkthrough"*); a **pitch video ≤2 min** (team, problem, why you); a **live product link** with access instructions and test logins for judges; an optional ≤30 s product ad (not judged).

## Judging (§5)
⚠️ **Two different rubrics are published.** Plan for both and ask organizers which applies:
| Rules §5.2 (all tracks, 20% each) | Track pages (every track) |
|---|---|
| Product Quality & Completeness | Technical Execution 20% |
| Technical Excellence | Design & Craft 20% |
| Monad Integration | Originality & Track Insight 15% |
| Track Fit & Problem Relevance | **Founder & Market Readiness 25%** |
| Innovation & Impact | Traction & Path Forward 20% |

Sponsor bounties: requirements adherence 40%, technical implementation 30%, Monad integration 20%, innovation 10%.
- **Submissions are reviewed non-confidentially.** Judges and mentors are investors and builders who *"may independently develop, fund, or acquire products similar to a submission"* (§5.3). Don't include anything secret.
- Decisions are final; no appeals; the organizer may choose not to award a prize if quality is too low.

## Prizes (§6)
Paid in **USDC or an equivalent stablecoin** within 30 days, to the team contact's wallet. **KYC may be required.** Winners handle their own taxes.
