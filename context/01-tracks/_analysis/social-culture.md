## Our analysis (not official; each claim cites its source)

### What the judges score (track page rubric)
The key question is whether *"participation or attention translate into real, verifiable economic stake… or is the onchain layer decorative?"* Founder & Market Readiness (25%) asks *"why that community would migrate from existing platforms."* Traction: *"even a small Discord or test group."*

### What stands out in the official ideas
- **5 of the 12 ideas are about trading card games (TCGs):** real-time order-book marketplace, player-governed TCGs, long-tail collectible AMMs, card rental/lending, cross-game assets. It's a strong signal of what the organizers want to see.
- Feed and algorithm ideas: algorithm marketplaces, community-governed recommendation engines, attention futures, early-supporter registries, wallet-seeded social graphs.
- Art: evolving generative art; "style as an ownable asset" with licensing and revenue splits.
