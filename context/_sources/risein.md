About this opportunity

Monad Metropolis Hackathon

Metropolis is Monad's six-week global online hackathon, a sprint to turn an idea into a working product on Monad and take it to a global stage. It's open to everyone, teams or solo, crypto-native or not. No prior onchain experience and no prior fundraising required. You build across one of four tracks: Onchain Finance & Trading, Consumer Products & Payments, Social/Attention & Culture, and Trust/Identity & AI Infrastructure. Total prize pool: $250,000+, with $30,000 per track, a $25,000 grand champion across all tracks, plus sponsor bounties (Agora, Nansen, Chainlink, Privy, Alibaba Cloud, Kimi and more). Top teams get ecosystem support and an invite to a residency program alongside other leading Monad teams.

## Tracks

- **Onchain Finance & Trading**

- **Consumer Products & Payments**

- **Social/Attention & Culture**

- **Trust/Identity & AI Infrastructure**

## Prizes

- **Total prize pool:** $250,000+

- **Per track:** $30,000 per track

- **Grand champion:** $25,000 grand champion across all tracks

- **Sponsor bounties:** Agora, Nansen, Chainlink, Privy, Alibaba Cloud, Kimi and more

## Eligibility

- **Who can participate:** Open to everyone

- **Team format:** Teams or solo

- **Background:** Crypto-native or not

- **Requirements:** No prior onchain experience and no prior fundraising required

## Timeline

- **Registration opens & build window starts:** Sep 1

- **Submission deadline:** Oct 13

- **Judging:** Oct 14 to 27

- **Winners announced:** Nov 3, 2026

## Format

Online, with one-day "Metropolis Lounge" activations in 7 cities (NYC, Hangzhou, San Francisco, London, Buenos Aires, Shenzhen, Singapore).

## Additional opportunities

- **Ecosystem support:** Top teams get ecosystem support

- **Residency program:** Top teams get an invite to a residency program alongside other leading Monad teams
