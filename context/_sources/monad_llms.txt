# Monad

> Monad is a high-performance, EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain with 10,000 TPS, 0.4 second block times, and 0.8 second single-slot finality. Monad reimagines every layer of the blockchain stack to deliver instant, scalable, and low-cost transactions for global payments, tokenization, and finance.

This site (monad.xyz) is the official home of the Monad Foundation. It hosts the public marketing site, official announcements, ecosystem and infrastructure directories, events, media, and legal/regulatory disclosures. Developer documentation lives at https://docs.monad.xyz.
