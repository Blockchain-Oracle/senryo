## Rules and eligibility (official FAQ on the marketing page)
- Open globally, to teams or solo builders. No prior onchain experience or fundraising is needed. Any country restrictions are in the "official rules on the application platform", behind login (not captured).
- **Existing projects are allowed "if the work you submit is new."** What you show on 13 Oct must have been built during the six weeks.
- Open source is **encouraged, not required**, but "judges need to be able to verify what you built during the six weeks." In practice: a public repo with dated commits, or a private repo shared with judges. One public repo already writes "Metropolis testnet build with attributed prior work" in its description. Copy that disclosure pattern.
- **Submission:** "a working product with a public project profile: a demo, a short write-up, and a link to the code." The project profile stays live after the event.
- **Tracks:**
  - You choose **one primary track** when you apply. It defines your main judging category.
  - You can then **add as many sponsor bounties as you like**. Each bounty lists the fields it needs, which you fill in at submission.
  - Bounties marked "All tracks" pair with any track. Others name a specific track.
  - Source: hackathon.monad.xyz/tracks.
  - "Choose one main track to qualify for any bounty. Main-track prizes are separate and stack with sponsor bounties" (/prizes).
- The platform "Project" workspace has four sections: Project Overview, Team, **Progress Updates** and Submission. The site description also mentions "milestones". Posting progress updates probably helps prove the work was new (inference).
- **Grand Champion:** "The single best project across all submissions and all four tracks, awarded at the judges' discretion."
- **Each track pays $10K each to 1st, 2nd and 3rd.**

## Judging criteria
- **No scoring rubric has been published** on the marketing page, Rise In, or the public parts of the platform. The platform FAQ ("Reading the FAQ", "Ask AI") and the official rules are behind login. The team should read them once logged in.
- Implicit criteria, from the official copy:
  - "Six weeks to build the next generation of **startups** on Monad."
  - "Built for teams that become startups."
  - The track "best fit" lines: shipped trading/lending before; care about "a user's first five minutes"; "have grown a community, not only built one"; comfortable with crypto, protocol design or agents.

## Follow-on programs (brief)
- **DeltaV** (https://deltav.monad.xyz): Monad's founder hub, offering credits, mentors, investor network and structured learning. The site shows 1.2K startups, 6.3K members and 723 startups across 129 cities, with 456 of those on Monad; it is multichain. Demo Day on 6 Oct 2026 in Singapore.
- **Monad Founder Residency** (https://residency.monad.xyz):
  - 3-week hybrid program: 1 week remote, then 2 weeks in person at Network School, Forest City, Malaysia.
  - Fully covered apart from travel. 10 projects per cohort. Demo Day with 200+ investors.
  - Past cohorts: Aug and Sep 2025. Cohort 2 selected 4 teams from 222 applicants, per SunDAO's LinkedIn post.
  - This is most likely the "residency" Metropolis winners are invited to (inference).
- **Nitro accelerator** (https://nitroacc.xyz): 3 months, 15 teams, **$500K per team** from Paradigm, Electric, Dragonfly and Castle Island, 1 month in NYC. First cohort was Apr 2026. Open to any chain.
- **Foundry**: 1–2 week in-person product acceleration in NYC, about 40 teams per cohort (Foundry NYC 3.3 ran 16–27 Mar 2026).
- **Mach**: early-stage accelerator (https://mach.monad.xyz).
- **The Studio NYC**: coworking (https://thestudio.monad.xyz).
- **Monad Madness**: pitch competition (https://madness.monad.xyz).
- Source for Nitro and Foundry: https://www.monad.xyz/blog/home-for-builders (2026-03-11).
