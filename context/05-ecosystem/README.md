## Top insights for choosing an idea

1. **The panel is VC-heavy and startup-framed.** Main-track judges are 16 people: 10 investors, 3 from the Monad Foundation, and 3 operators (Nansen, Eigen, Layer3). The official copy says "next generation of startups". Past winners were consumer-legible products that became companies. Pitch traction, a wedge and a business model, not just the tech.
2. **Stablecoin consumer payments is the strongest shared signal among judges.** Examples: AUSD, passkeys (Mera), gasless UX. But Track 2 remittance and payment links are already crowded (at least 7 public repos). Pick an under-served prompt instead: per-second subscriptions (Sablier is idle on Monad) or group settle-up (nothing live).
3. **Track 4 is the most crowded.** At least 10 public repos are ERC-8004 "agent passport, reputation or receipt" projects. The whitespace is media provenance that survives re-encoding, and passkey products built on Mera, which Monad pays $5K in bounties for.
4. **In Track 1, Monad's real activity is on its CLOB**: Kuru did $2.87B in 30 days, while perps TVL is only $7.8M. Onchain-credit-history lending has no incumbents (Accountable holds $93K). Per-block-funding perps and new Kuru markets are open, and they stack with the Kuru and Perpl bounties.
5. **Track 3 has almost no public competitors.** Monad's own taste runs to markets on culture (CRSH, Kizzy, Blink.cash, NoJohns). The catch is that it needs visible users or a community.
6. **Stack bounties.** One primary track plus unlimited sponsor bounties. For example, Agora's $10K mobile trading bounty, Kuru's $5K bounties and the Mera passkey bounties can all apply to a single product.
