## 4. What this panel rewards

1. **Stablecoins that move real money (T2).** This is the loudest signal on the panel: Galaxy, Castle Island, Electric, CoinFund, Dragonfly, Eunice and Primitive all back it. The winning shape is stablecoin payments, cards, remittance or savings for non-crypto users, especially in emerging markets. Build on MetaMask mUSD, Money Account and Mercuryo ramps. Dragonfly's "stablecoin cards up 1000%" and Castle Island's EM thesis are the framing.
2. **Market structure over another DEX fork (T1).** Frankie, Tommy Hang, Joey Shin, Max Tang and the Perpl/Category Labs judges will reward CLOB/perps innovation that only Monad's speed makes viable: PLOB, Omnibook, liquidation-free leverage, equity or commodity perps. Show tighter spreads and deeper OI, not TVL claims.
3. **Prediction markets are the crossover bet (T1 + T3).** Paradigm, Archetype, Dragonfly, Electric (PM-based insurance), IOSG (PM agents) and Galaxy all call it out. Go for a novel market design or a consumer or cultural wedge; a Polymarket clone will lose ("most copycats fail").
4. **Agents that actually transact onchain (T4 + T1).** Nansen ("agents as the primary trading interface"), Archetype (agentic curators, agent-first UIs), Galaxy (AI payments onchain) and Pantera (AI co-pilots). Pair an agent with **verifiability** (Eigen: TEE, deterministic inference, attestation; IOSG portfolio: ZK/FHE/TEE) and with MetaMask delegation or Agent Wallet for scoped permissions.
5. **User-owned, privacy-preserving identity and trust (T4).** Electric's 26-ideas frame (control, privacy, access) and Eigen's verifiable-compute stack. Credible-neutral infrastructure echoes Keone's op-ed.
6. **Consumer UX with honest risk (T2 + T3).** Prelude ("for everyone"), 6MV (N-of-1 consumer), Zeel (Ample prize-linked savings, "don't hide what could go wrong") and Eunice (beyond crypto-natives). Gamified finance and creator commerce with micropayments (Archetype) fit best. Use gasless flows, embedded wallets and fiat ramps.
7. **PMF evidence over vapor (all tracks).** Tina's Foundry, Pantera's "brutal pruning" and Evan Feng's fundamentals lens all point one way: show real users, retention or revenue logic, and a path to being a company. Use Monad-native performance meaningfully (Category Labs, Keone), and add sponsor integrations (Alchemy, Quicknode, Envio, Dynamic) for bounty stacking.
8. **Social and culture are the thinnest part of the panel (T3).** No main-track judge champions pure social. Frame T3 entries through finance x entertainment (Electric), prediction/attention markets, or creator payments (Archetype) to land with this panel.
